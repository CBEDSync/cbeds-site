What you can do here

One space, three ways to take part

Sense making is a shared activity. CBEDSense brings the conversation, the signal and the work into one place.

Discuss topics

Open threads on standards, methods and ideas. Ask questions, challenge assumptions and learn from across the ecosystem.

Share news & updates

Post what is happening: new releases, consultations, events and milestones, so the signal reaches the people who need it.

Showcase your work

Add your projects, tools and outputs for the community to see, use and review. Good work becomes visible and reusable.

The space

Make sense of the ecosystem

An acronym comes up in a meeting and everyone nods. Afterwards you try to pin it down: standard or product, who is behind it, who else uses it. The answers exist, scattered across websites and slide decks.

That is what this space is for. Ask in your own words and the answer is assembled from CBEDSync itself, not the open web. Under it sits today's insight from the same data, and the latest from the community.
